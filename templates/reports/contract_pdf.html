Binding Legal Terms, Conditions & Liability Clauses

1. OPERATING HOURS & OVERTIME HOUR-METER RULES: The agreed rental tariff permits standard machine operation of up to eight (8) operating hours per single working shift per calendar day. Operating hours recorded on the machinery hour-meter exceeding this threshold shall be deemed excess overtime hours and billed to the Lessee at the standard overtime hourly tariff on each periodic invoice or upon final return settlement.
2. MACHINE OPERATION, SUPERVISION & FUEL RESPONSIBILITY: The Lessee warrants that machinery will be operated strictly by licensed, qualified, and competent operators in accordance with manufacturer technical guidelines and factory load thresholds. Machinery is dispatched with a full tank of specified clean fuel. The Lessee must return the machinery with a full fuel tank; failing which, refueling will be billed at prevailing commercial retail rates plus a 15% administrative service surcharge. Daily preventative inspections, lubricant top-ups, tire/track visual monitoring, and coolant checks are the direct responsibility of the Lessee.
3. BREAKDOWN & MAINTENANCE LIABILITY CLAUSES: (a) The Lessor (CERMS) remains responsible for routine scheduled preventative maintenance and wear-and-tear repairs resulting from ordinary, prudent machine operation.
(b) In the event of mechanical breakdown, malfunction, or structural failure, the Lessee must cease operation immediately and notify CERMS within two (2) hours. Continued operation following an alarm, fluid leak, or warning signal shall constitute gross negligence.
(c) Any damage or breakdown arising from operator error, hazardous site ground conditions, overloading, contaminated fuel, physical impact, roll-over, or unauthorized third-party repairs is the sole financial and operational liability of the Lessee. Repair costs, replacement parts, and downtime rental charges will be charged to the Lessee or deducted from the held security deposit.
4. SITE SECURITY, INDEMNIFICATION & THIRD-PARTY RISK: The Lessee shall maintain 24-hour physical security over the equipment at the project site. The Lessee indemnifies and holds harmless CERMS, its officers, and employees against any third-party claims, legal actions, property damage, bodily injury, environmental spill remediation, or wrongful death occurring during or connected with the custody, storage, or operation of the leased machinery.
5. ESCROW SECURITY DEPOSIT & SETTLEMENT RECONCILIATION: The security deposit of Rs. {{ contract.deposit_paid|floatformat:2 }} is held in escrow by CERMS. Upon demobilization, a joint Physical Dispatch & Return Inspection shall be conducted. CERMS will issue a Final Commercial Tax Invoice reconciling accrued rental fees, excess operating hours, fuel variances, and damage recovery charges, offset against the escrow security deposit. Any remaining credit balance will be refunded within seven (7) banking days.
6. TITLE, OWNERSHIP & RIGHT OF REPOSSESSION: Title and ownership of the machinery remain exclusively with CERMS at all times. This Agreement constitutes a pure lease and creates no equitable interest, lien, or ownership option for the Lessee. In the event of default on payment, breach of maintenance covenants, or insolvency, CERMS reserves the immediate right to enter the Lessee's site and repossess the machinery without judicial process or prior notice.
